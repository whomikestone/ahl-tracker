# What's new

## October 31, 2026
- On team pages, tap a game's score to see who scored: each goal by period, with the time, the scorer, the assists, the running score, and whether it was a power-play, shorthanded, empty-net, or penalty-shot goal. If the details aren't available for a game, there's a link to its official AHL game summary instead.
- Upcoming games now show the puck-drop time in your own time zone: on team pages (in a new Time column), in the team card's next game, and in the next three games when you tap a team. Times appear after the next nightly update.

## October 24, 2026
- Added team pages. Pick a team from the "Your team" list and choose "Full season and schedule", or open a team's row in any table. Each page shows every game so far, newest first, with the score, the result (W, L, OTL or SOL), the points earned, and the season record after each game. It also lists the games still to play and the team's home and road records.
- Tapping a team in a table now also shows its next three games.

## October 17, 2026
- Added RW (regulation wins) and ROW (regulation and overtime wins) columns. They're the AHL's first tiebreakers, so they explain the order of tied teams.
- Records now use the AHL's four-number format everywhere: W-L-OTL-SOL.
- Tables now show every column on every device. On phones, swipe sideways to see more; the rank, movement arrow, and team stay pinned on the left.
- Shootout losses are now labeled SOL instead of OTL in the team dropdowns.
- Added "What the columns mean" under each table, including why the order can differ from sites that sort by total points.
- Added this page.

## October 11, 2026
- Updated for the 2026-27 realignment: Bridgeport moved to Hamilton and joined the North, so the Atlantic now has 7 teams and the North 8. Playoff spots and byes now follow each division's size.
- Team logos are now served from this site, so they load reliably and stay current.
- Split the site into separate pages: Divisions, Conferences, League, Playoff odds, and Trends.

## October 4, 2026
- First version: standings by division, conference, and league, playoff odds, weekly movement arrows, a week-by-week trends chart, and a favorite team card.
